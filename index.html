Welcome to EquityNote Master

Purpose

This tool is designed to parse contract notes from multiple brokers and convert them into a structured format.

🎯 Three Modes: Anand Rathi Equity - Delivery/Intraday trades with ISIN Anand Rathi F&O - Futures & Options trades Motilal Oswal - Motilal Oswal equity trades

How to Use

📂
1

Select the broker tab (Anand Rathi Equity, F&O, or Motilal Oswal)

📄
2

Click "Select Files" to choose .txt files

▶️
3

Click "Import" to parse the data

📊
4

Export to Excel or CSV
